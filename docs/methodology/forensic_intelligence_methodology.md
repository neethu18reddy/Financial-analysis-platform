## 1. Governance & Zero-Accusation Standard

All forensic indicators and signals generated by this platform are **probabilistic statistical screening signals** designed for institutional investment diligence. 

### Mandatory Stance Guardrails
1. **Never State Proof of Fraud**: High risk flags indicate anomalous statistical distributions, accounting aggressiveness, or structural distress—never intentional malfeasance or legal non-compliance.
2. **Neutral Descriptive Vocabulary**: The platform strictly enforces standardized descriptive taxonomy:
   - `ANOMALY` / `DIVERGENCE` / `SCREENING_SIGNAL` / `POTENTIAL_CONCERN` / `REQUIRES_INVESTIGATION`
3. **No Autonomous Accusations**: LLM agents are prohibited from translating statistical metric outliers into factual misconduct claims.
4. **Transparent Lineage**: Every score exposes raw variables, underlying formula coefficients, accounting standard assumptions, and source provenance.

## 2. Beneish M-Score (Earnings Manipulation Detection)

### Standard 8-Variable Linear Model
$$\text{M-Score} = -4.84 + (0.920 \times \text{DSRI}) + (0.528 \times \text{GMI}) + (0.404 \times \text{AQI}) + (0.892 \times \text{SGI}) + (0.115 \times \text{DEPI}) - (0.172 \times \text{SGAI}) + (4.037 \times \text{TATA}) + (0.0327 \times \text{LVGI})$$

### Variables Definition & Indian Accounting (Ind AS) Mapping:
1. **DSRI (Days Sales in Receivables Index)**:
   $$\text{DSRI} = \frac{\text{Receivables}_t / \text{Revenue}_t}{\text{Receivables}_{t-1} / \text{Revenue}_{t-1}}$$
   *Signal*: Disproportionate spike in receivables relative to revenue (potential channel stuffing / aggressive revenue recognition).
2. **GMI (Gross Margin Index)**:
   $$\text{GMI} = \frac{\text{Gross Margin}_{t-1}}{\text{Gross Margin}_t}$$
   *Signal*: Deteriorating gross margins create heightened pressure for earnings management.
3. **AQI (Asset Quality Index)**:
   $$\text{Non-Current Assets} = \text{Total Assets} - (\text{Current Assets} + \text{PPE} + \text{Securities})$$
   $$\text{AQI} = \frac{1 - (\text{Current Assets}_t + \text{PPE}_t) / \text{Total Assets}_t}{1 - (\text{Current Assets}_{t-1} + \text{PPE}_{t-1}) / \text{Total Assets}_{t-1}}$$
   *Signal*: Capitalization of non-tangible operating expenses into intangibles or other non-current assets.
4. **SGI (Sales Growth Index)**:
   $$\text{SGI} = \frac{\text{Revenue}_t}{\text{Revenue}_{t-1}}$$
5. **DEPI (Depreciation Index)**:
   $$\text{Depreciation Rate} = \frac{\text{Depreciation}}{\text{PPE Gross} + \text{Depreciation}}$$
   $$\text{DEPI} = \frac{\text{Depreciation Rate}_{t-1}}{\text{Depreciation Rate}_t}$$
   *Signal*: Slowdown in depreciation rate indicating extension of asset useful life.
6. **SGAI (Sales General & Administrative Expenses Index)**:
   $$\text{SGAI} = \frac{\text{SGA}_t / \text{Revenue}_t}{\text{SGA}_{t-1} / \text{Revenue}_{t-1}}$$
7. **LVGI (Leverage Index)**:
   $$\text{LVGI} = \frac{\text{Total Long-Term Debt}_t / \text{Total Assets}_t}{\text{Total Long-Term Debt}_{t-1} / \text{Total Assets}_{t-1}}$$
8. **TATA (Total Accruals to Total Assets)**:
   $$\text{TATA} = \frac{\text{Net Income (PAT)}_t - \text{CFO}_t}{\text{Total Assets}_t}$$

### Classification Thresholds:
- **$\text{M-Score} > -1.78$**: `HIGH_RISK` / `ANOMALOUS` (Statistical distribution resembles past manipulation samples).
- **$\text{M-Score} \le -1.78$**: `LOW_RISK` / `NORMAL` (Unlikely earnings manipulation).

### Applicability Guardrails:
- **Banking / Financial Institutions**: `NOT_APPLICABLE` (Banking balance sheets do not segment trade receivables/gross margins in corporate format; model would produce misleading values).

## 3. Piotroski 9-Point F-Score (Fundamental Health Screening)

Binary scoring matrix assessing directional improvement across 3 core pillars:

### Pillar 1: Profitability (4 Points)
1. **Positive Net Income**: $\text{PAT}_t > 0 \implies +1$
2. **Positive Operating Cash Flow**: $\text{CFO}_t > 0 \implies +1$
3. **Return on Assets YoY Improvement**: $\text{ROA}_t > \text{ROA}_{t-1} \implies +1$
4. **Cash Quality Superiority (Quality of Earnings)**: $\text{CFO}_t > \text{PAT}_t \implies +1$

### Pillar 2: Leverage, Liquidity & Source of Funds (3 Points)
5. **Deleveraging (Lower Long-Term Debt Ratio)**: $\text{LTDebt}_t / \text{Assets}_t \le \text{LTDebt}_{t-1} / \text{Assets}_{t-1} \implies +1$
6. **Liquidity Expansion (Higher Current Ratio)**: $\text{Current Ratio}_t > \text{Current Ratio}_{t-1} \implies +1$
7. **Zero Equity Dilution (No New Shares Issued)**: $\text{Shares Outstanding}_t \le \text{Shares Outstanding}_{t-1} \implies +1$

### Pillar 3: Operating Efficiency (2 Points)
8. **Gross Margin Expansion**: $\text{Gross Margin \%}_t > \text{Gross Margin \%}_{t-1} \implies +1$
9. **Asset Turnover Improvement**: $\frac{\text{Revenue}_t}{\text{Assets}_t} > \frac{\text{Revenue}_{t-1}}{\text{Assets}_{t-1}} \implies +1$

### Classification:
- **Score 8 – 9**: `STRONG` (Highest quality fundamental health & solvency).
- **Score 5 – 7**: `MODERATE` / `STABLE` (Typical corporate performance).
- **Score 0 – 4**: `WEAK` / `DISTRESSED` (Underperforming, fundamental deterioration).

## 4. Altman Emerging Market Z''-Score (Solvency & Distress)

### Formulation for Emerging Markets (Indian Corporate Environment):
$$\text{Z''-Score} = 6.56(X_1) + 3.26(X_2) + 6.72(X_3) + 1.05(X_4)$$

Where:
- $X_1 = \frac{\text{Working Capital}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (Short-term liquidity health)
- $X_2 = \frac{\text{Retained Earnings}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (Cumulative profitability & self-funding ability)
- $X_3 = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (Core asset productivity independent of leverage & taxes)
- $X_4 = \frac{\text{Book Value of Equity}}{\text{Total Liabilities}}$ (Solvency buffer against debt)

### Emerging Market Zones:
- **$Z'' > 2.60$**: `SAFE_ZONE` (Negligible default risk).
- **$1.10 \le Z'' \le 2.60$**: `GREY_ZONE` (Moderate risk, vigilance advised).
- **$Z'' < 1.10$**: `DISTRESS_ZONE` (Elevated financial distress risk).

| `SIG_TAX_RATE_ANOMALY`| Effective Tax Rate Divergence | $\frac{\text{Tax Expense}}{\text{PBT}}$ | Rate $< 12\%$ or $> 40\%$ (Ind AS benchmark: $\sim 25.17\%$) |

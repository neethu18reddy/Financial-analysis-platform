# SEBI Compliance, Analytical Limitations & Research Disclaimers

## 1. Regulatory Context (SEBI RA Regulations 2014)

1. **Non-Advisory Nature**: This platform and its AI Analyst subsystem operate as quantitative decision intelligence and fundamental screening software. It does not constitute investment advice, personalized portfolio management, or a buy/sell recommendation.
2. **Methodology Transparency**: All valuation ranges, intrinsic values, and forensic scores are accompanied by explicit input assumptions, discount rates, terminal growth rates, and model limitations.
3. **No Representation of Certainty**: Forward projections and DCF valuations are probabilistic scenario estimates subject to market and macroeconomic volatility.

---

## 2. Statistical Screening Model Limitations

- **Beneish M-Score**: Statistical screening model based on historical US corporate restatements; elevated scores in fast-growing Indian enterprises often reflect organic business expansion rather than aggressive earnings manipulation.
- **Altman Z-Score**: Originally calibrated on traditional capital-intensive manufacturing entities. Inapplicable to banks, NBFCs, and asset-light technology service providers.
- **Piotroski F-Score**: Binary test sensitive to temporary cyclical fluctuations in working capital and liquidity.

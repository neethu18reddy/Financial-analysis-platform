# Annual Report Ingestion, Copyright & Terms Compliance

**Version**: `1.0.0-phase6`  
**Applicability**: Public Corporate Disclosures & Statutory Exchange Filings (NSE / BSE / MCA)

---

## 1. Regulatory Context of Indian Corporate Filings

Under Section 136 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 34 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, all listed companies in India are statutory obligated to make their complete annual reports, financial statements, and auditor reports publicly accessible to shareholders and market participants.

These filings are submitted electronically via:
- National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) NEAPS Portal
- BSE India Corporate Announcement Platform
- Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA21)

---

## 2. Ingestion & Analytical Terms

1. **Public Domain & Fair Dealing**: Corporate annual reports and statutory filings ingested by this platform are analyzed under the doctrine of fair dealing for research, valuation, and financial education purposes.
2. **Provenance & Attribution**: The platform does not alter original authorial statements. Every extracted excerpt carries explicit attribution to the source document, fiscal year, publishing entity, and physical page number.
3. **No Fabrication Guarantee**: The system prohibits synthetic generation or AI fabrication of corporate disclosures. If a disclosure is not present in the indexed filing, the engine explicitly reports no matching evidence found.
4. **Source URLs & Archival**: Where provided, original public URLs from company investor relations portals or stock exchange archives are preserved in the metadata registry.
